# Tariff Refund Agency — Agent Training Hub

## Three Visual Directions

### 1. Operations Command Deck
**Very Brief Intro:** A confidential-feeling internal cockpit for agents, informed by aerospace operations manuals and premium financial workspaces. Structured, calm, and focused on progressing through a defined learning path.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Global Trade Fieldbook
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile, editorial training field guide inspired by port ledgers, customs documentation, and trade maps. It feels intelligent and grounded in the work agents will actually do.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Academy Signal System
**Very Brief Intro:** A bold modular training environment that uses indexed modules, progress markers, and signal colors to turn complex material into a clear operating playbook.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen Direction: Operations Command Deck

### Design Movement
**Institutional futurism**, blending premium fintech dashboards with operations control rooms. The experience is intentionally agent-facing: it should feel like a secure workspace for building mastery, not a public acquisition landing page.

### Core Principles
1. **Orientation before action:** The current lesson, role, and next recommended step remain obvious everywhere.
2. **Structured confidence:** Dense legal and process material is turned into short, purposeful cards, checkpoints, and reference panels.
3. **Credible visual restraint:** Navy, gold, paper white, and limited signal colors communicate authority without promotional excess.
4. **Deliberate progression:** The content is indexed as an operational sequence instead of a long marketing scroll.

### Color Philosophy
The signature field is **Command Navy**, an authoritative near-black blue that recalls customs, shipping, and executive operations. Warm **Recovery Gold** is reserved for the active module, key decisions, and successful next steps, so it signals value without becoming decoration. Fogged blue-gray surfaces allow information density while preserving calm, and muted copper hints at ports, paperwork, and legacy trade systems.

### Layout Paradigm
The site operates as a **boxed mission console**. A fixed utility rail anchors the learning index on wider screens; the main workspace is a tall editorial column composed of nested panels. Instead of a centered marketing layout, the content moves from a mission briefing through operational modules, scripted conversations, tool references, and a controlled knowledge check.

### Signature Elements
1. A numbered vertical **mission index** with active-state markers and visible completion bars.
2. Fine blueprint-grid texture on navy panels, suggesting systems, logistics, and precision.
3. Gold **signal bars** and compact status chips marking “Learn,” “Practice,” and “Reference” states.

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should reduce uncertainty. Section navigation scrolls precisely to a module, the hero carousel changes the current briefing, and learning cards expand only when agents request the supporting detail. Buttons use imperative, internal language such as “Open Talk Track” rather than public-sales language.

### Animation
Motion is understated and task-oriented. The active index marker glides to its new module, hero briefing content crossfades, and panels enter with a short upward fade using a crisp custom ease. Hover treatments sharpen borders and slightly lift cards. All non-essential animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Manrope** provides sharp, highly legible interface typography for operational labels and body copy. **DM Sans** in heavier weights drives concise, human-readable module headlines. The hierarchy uses small tracked uppercase labels, large compact headings, and relaxed supporting copy; nothing should feel like a consumer ad.

### Brand Essence
**Tariff Refund Agency’s internal operating academy for agents who need a clear, credible path from tariff question to qualified referral.**

**Personality:** Precise, reassuring, commercially aware.

### Brand Voice
The voice is direct, capable, and peer-to-peer. Headlines tell the agent what to learn or do next; CTAs identify the resource or action without hype.

> “Know the path before you open the conversation.”

> “Use the eligibility signal to decide what happens next.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied TRA mark as the primary wordmark. Its sharp diagonal slash and gold shield become the visual cue for directional movement, completion, and protected process.

### Signature Brand Color
**Recovery Gold — #F4B41A.** It is the ownable visual signal for confirmed opportunity, active learning, and next-step action.

## Style Decisions

- The supplied opportunity-field-guide reference is the visual benchmark: a deep navy operating field, teal signal accents, oversized compact headlines, compact evidence panels, and indexed navigation.
- The portal will remain internally positioned. Client-facing applications and funding forms are deliberately excluded from the core learning path.
- The Tariff Hub handoff establishes the primary module set: overview, business script, broker path, objections and safe responses, ICP/category cues, resource standards, and controlled practice.
- Legal, tariff, eligibility, funding, recovery-result, deadline, and compensation claims require owner or counsel approval and a current review date before they can appear as operative training content.
- Recovery Gold `#F4B41A` is the primary advancement color for active modules, confirmed next steps, and main calls to action; teal is reserved for diagnostic signals and qualification markers.
- The TRA slash-and-shield geometry is repeated in progress bars, active navigation, action controls, and card edges as the academy’s directional, protected-process motif.
- Trade operations, customs evidence, secure review, documentation, ports, freight, and specialist handoff environments are the preferred imagery world; generic meeting scenes must be operationally framed.
- The hero is intentionally compact and image-led. Its headline remains decisive but cannot consume the full first viewport; the supplied opportunity carousel acts as the visual mission brief.
- The complete 18-category research area is a color-coded prospecting system. Every card uses a category-specific signal color and gains a controlled glow, lift, and edge accent on hover.
- TRA slash-and-shield geometry is now the persistent movement and protection motif in rail markers, progress rails, section status cues, and category card edges.
- Category cards use a restrained sector-color system rather than a rainbow palette. They must read as data tiles: signal color first, category title second, supporting examples third.
- Major modules carry an explicit next-module cue so the agent remains oriented through the full command-deck sequence.
- Navigation lives across the top as an active-state module strip. The recovered canvas space belongs to the opportunity carousel, which should be the primary visual device in the hero rather than a secondary panel.
- Carousel artwork is display-first: it must use `contain` within its 16:9 stage so the supplied visual story is fully visible, never cropped at the top.
- The category explorer uses vertically structured operational cards with distinct code, sector signal, title, concise examples, and one direct next action. A floating module navigator provides previous/next travel through the page.
