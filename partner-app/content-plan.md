# Tariff Alliance Network — Content and Media Plan

## Core Proposition

**Your relationships already open doors. Now they can unlock refunds.**

Tariff Alliance Network helps customs brokers, CPAs, chambers of commerce, professional agents, and entrepreneurs introduce eligible U.S. importers to a compliance-first tariff-refund recovery workflow. Partners stay focused on relationships; the recovery team coordinates intake, documentation, data review, and the appropriate professional handoffs.

## Primary Calls to Action

The master CTA is **Choose Your Alliance Route** and scrolls to the alliance-path selector. Customs-broker CTAs link to `https://tariffsolutionsgroup.com/brokers/signup?ref=ceo`. CPA, chamber, agent, and entrepreneur CTAs link to `https://tariffsolutionsgroup.com/login?tab=signup&ref=ceo`.

## Shared Opportunity

**Section headline:** `The opportunity is complex. The partnership does not have to be.`

Partners do not need to interpret tariff law, analyze every ACE record, predict outcomes, or manage filings. Their role is to recognize potential fit, introduce the importer through a tracked pathway, and remain the trusted relationship owner while specialists coordinate the recovery workflow.

### System Modules

**Discover:** Identify importers in an existing network and share a compliant, educational entry point.

**Connect:** Use a custom partner link or co-branded pathway so every introduction is attributed accurately.

**Coordinate:** The recovery team organizes intake, documents, entry data, milestones, and professional handoffs.

**Track:** Partners maintain visibility into introduced opportunities and next-step status through the partner process.

## Partner Economics

**Section headline:** `Create client value without creating an operations department.`

The business case is expressed through four non-numeric value pillars rather than guaranteed earnings claims: new service-line potential, stronger client retention, zero internal buildout, and transparent introduction tracking. Any compensation depends on the signed partner agreement, qualified introductions, completed recovery outcomes, and applicable compliance requirements.

## Protected Relationship

**Section headline:** `Your client. Your credibility. Your seat at the table.`

The site emphasizes co-branded and white-label options, no solicitation for unrelated entry, freight, or compliance work, structured status visibility, and compliance-first language that avoids unsupported promises. Customs brokers receive a dedicated pathway because operational and client-protection concerns are different from conventional referral partners.

## Activation Sequence

1. **Select your route.** Choose the pathway that matches how you serve importer businesses.
2. **Align the model.** Review the agreement, relationship protections, branding preference, and compliant talking points.
3. **Launch your portal.** Receive a tracked link and a ready-to-use introduction path.
4. **Activate the network.** Educate importer relationships, introduce potential opportunities, and follow progress.

## Interactive Fit Check

The visitor selects one of five identities. The interface returns a tailored recommendation, promise, proof points, and the correct external signup CTA. This feature is advisory only and does not assess importer refund eligibility.

## FAQ Topics

The accordion covers who the program fits, whether tariff expertise is required, how client relationships are protected, how attribution works, whether partners can promise outcomes, what happens after an introduction, and the difference between the broker pathway and the general strategic-alliance pathway.

## Compliance Footer

Tariff Alliance Network is a strategic-partnership education and introduction experience. It is not CBP, U.S. Customs, a government agency, a law firm, an accounting firm, or a customs broker. It does not provide legal, tax, accounting, customs-brokerage, or financial advice. Refund eligibility, filing options, timing, funding, recovery amounts, and partner compensation depend on specific facts, documentation, agreements, applicable law, and third-party review. No refund, approval, funding, timeline, or compensation is guaranteed.

## Cinematic Partner Video

### Format

A 75–90 second 16:9 cinematic briefing with full-color international-trade visuals, on-screen route graphics, energetic instrumental music, and concise narration. It should feel like a premium alliance launch film rather than a conventional explainer.

### Storyboard and Narration

**Scene 1 — Global movement / 0–10 seconds**

Visuals: Container port at sunrise, cranes, cargo movement, bright cyan route line moving across the frame.

Narration: “Across the country, importer relationships are carrying more than goods. They may be carrying overlooked tariff-refund opportunities.”

On-screen copy: `THE OPPORTUNITY IS ALREADY IN YOUR NETWORK`

**Scene 2 — Trusted advisors / 10–24 seconds**

Visuals: Customs broker reviewing entries, CPA meeting with a business owner, chamber roundtable, professional advisor making an introduction.

Narration: “Customs brokers, CPAs, chambers, professional agents, and entrepreneurs are already trusted by the businesses that need to understand what may be recoverable.”

On-screen copy: `BROKERS · CPAs · CHAMBERS · AGENTS · ENTREPRENEURS`

**Scene 3 — Complexity becomes a system / 24–42 seconds**

Visuals: Organized documentation, dashboards, secure portal patterns, specialists collaborating. Animated color routes converge into one network symbol.

Narration: “You do not need to become a tariff expert or build a recovery department. Make the introduction. The recovery workflow coordinates intake, documents, data review, and the right professional handoffs.”

On-screen copy: `DISCOVER → CONNECT → COORDINATE → TRACK`

**Scene 4 — Relationship protection / 42–58 seconds**

Visuals: Two professionals side by side with an importer client; co-branded portal mockup; shield-like route frame.

Narration: “Your relationship stays central. Choose co-branded or specialist-led pathways, maintain visibility, and lead with compliant language that protects your credibility.”

On-screen copy: `YOUR CLIENT · YOUR CREDIBILITY · YOUR SEAT AT THE TABLE`

**Scene 5 — Five routes / 58–72 seconds**

Visuals: Five saturated colors form separate lines, then interlock. Each audience name appears as a destination marker.

Narration: “Five alliance routes. One shared objective: create more value for importer businesses through a structured, relationship-first opportunity.”

On-screen copy: `CHOOSE YOUR ALLIANCE ROUTE`

**Scene 6 — Final call / 72–85 seconds**

Visuals: Interlocked bridge-arrow symbol over a luminous Atlantic-blue field; route lines continue toward the viewer.

Narration: “If your network includes U.S. importers, your next strategic partnership may already be waiting. Activate your route.”

On-screen copy: `TARIFF ALLIANCE NETWORK / NETWORK STATUS: OPEN`

### Audio Direction

The music should combine modern cinematic percussion, precise electronic pulses, warm brass swells, and a confident final lift. The narrator should sound authoritative, collaborative, and energetic—not aggressive or alarmist.
