# Internal Training Curriculum

The portal is a **sales-enablement workspace for authorized Tariff Refund Agency representatives**. It is not a public client website and does not make eligibility decisions, tariff-recovery claims, legal interpretations, financial promises, or case-result claims.

## Learning Path

| Module | Agent outcome | Site treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Understand the conversation path and the reason for a qualification-first approach. | Hero briefing, guided next step, and operational status panel. |
| Opportunity Field Guide | Identify operational signals for appropriate conversations without deciding legal eligibility. | Qualification map, discovery questions, and escalation note. |
| Business Script | Open, discover, frame a next step, and close with compliant language. | Sequential talk-track cards with copy prompts. |
| Broker Script | Position TRA as a partner while respecting the broker relationship. | Partnership conversation stages and boundary reminders. |
| Categories | Recognize import-adjacent business models worth researching further. | Industry cards with suggested questions, not eligibility claims. |
| Qualifier | Run a short, controlled conversation screen and choose the appropriate next action. | Four-step practice flow and reference panel. |
| Practice Room | Rehearse calls using safe scenario cards rather than live legal or financial advice. | Scenario selector and coaching standards. |

## Content Controls

All time-sensitive, legal, regulatory, tariff, recovery-result, deadline, compensation, customer-story, or approval-status content must be separately reviewed before publication. This first release deliberately uses training mechanics, placeholders for approved language, and clear escalation cues in place of unverified claims.

> **Internal training guidance only.** Materials do not provide legal, tax, customs, financial, or professional advice. Refer questions about eligibility, program terms, law, deadlines, funding, or recovery outcomes to approved qualified professionals and current program materials.
