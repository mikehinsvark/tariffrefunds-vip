## Recommended Training-Site Integration

The next logical routes are **Business Script**, **Contact Research**, and **Comp Plan & Calculator Reference**. The first two can be added as internal training modules using approved wording. The compensation and calculator material should be added only after the current plan, rates, thresholds, assumptions, and disclosures are owner-approved, preferably as a separately routed internal calculator reference rather than static income claims.

## Content-Control Boundary

Do not add deadline, litigation, tariff legality, recovery, result, fee, funding, income, title, payout, bonus, or eligibility claims from the source sites without a current approved version and an owner-confirmed effective date.
